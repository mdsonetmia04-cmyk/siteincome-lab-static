Nintendo Black Friday Deals 2026: How Gaming Blogs Earn From Deal Season

November 13, 20265 min readBy Sonet Mia

Nintendo officially announced its Black Friday 2026 deals on November 12 — and searches for Nintendo deals jumped immediately. The headline offer is a Nintendo Switch 2 + Mario Kart World bundle for $499.99, $50 off its usual price, available November 22 through December 5, including a three-month Nintendo Switch Online membership.

For gaming bloggers, the announcement is the starting gun. Deal season is the highest-earning stretch of the year for gaming content — if the posts are ready before the shoppers arrive.

The Official Nintendo Black Friday 2026 Deals

All of the following comes from Nintendo's official announcement. Prices are US suggested retail prices, and offers last while supplies last:

Console bundles (November 22 – December 5):

  • Nintendo Switch 2 + Mario Kart World Bundle — $499.99 ($50 off). Includes the console, a full download of Mario Kart World, and a 3-month Nintendo Switch Online Individual Membership.
  • Nintendo Switch 2 + Nintendo Switch Sports Resort Bundle — $529.99. Includes the console, a full download of the game, and the same 3-month membership.

Games $20 off (November 22 – November 29) at Amazon, Best Buy, GameStop, Target, Walmart, and Nintendo's New York and San Francisco stores:

  • Donkey Kong Bananza (Switch 2)
  • Metroid Prime 4: Beyond (Switch 2 and Switch)
  • Kirby Air Riders (Switch 2)
  • Super Mario Party Jamboree – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Jamboree TV
  • Super Mario Galaxy + Super Mario Galaxy 2 (Switch)

eShop sale (November 19 – December 2): A sale on select digital games on Nintendo eShop and the Nintendo Store. Nintendo has not yet revealed which titles or discounts will be included.

Two honest notes: the standalone Switch 2 console itself is not discounted — the savings come from the bundles. And prices and stock can change quickly during Black Friday week, so readers should always check the retailer's page before buying. Black Friday itself falls on November 27, 2026.

Why Deal Season Pays Better Than Normal Gaming Content

Deal content earns more than reviews and news for three reasons:

  1. Shoppers search with their wallets open. Someone searching "Nintendo Black Friday deals" is ready to spend. Advertisers pay premium rates to reach buyers, which means higher ad revenue per visitor on deal pages.
  2. Deal posts get shared. A good deal roundup travels through social media, group chats, and forums in a way a review rarely does. Every share is free traffic.
  3. Retailers do not answer the real question. Amazon tells you the price. A blog tells you whether the deal is actually good, which bundle is worth it, and what to skip. That judgment is what readers search for — and what Google rewards.

Nintendo rarely discounts its own games, which makes a $20 price cut on first-party titles a genuine news event. Deal posts about rare discounts outperform generic "best deals" lists every time.

The Deal-Season Playbook for Gaming Blogs

Publish on announcement day. Nintendo announced on November 12; the sales start November 19 and 22. The blogs that publish first get indexed first and linked first. Waiting until Black Friday week means competing with every major outlet at once.

One living post, updated daily. Keep a single deal post and update it as prices change, stock runs out, and the eShop titles are revealed. Update the timestamp with each change. One strong, current post outranks ten stale ones.

Answer "which one should I buy?" Compare the two bundles directly: the Mario Kart World bundle at $499.99 is the better value for most buyers, since it matches the standalone console's price while adding a full game and a membership. Direct recommendations are what turn searchers into loyal readers.

Cover the whole season, not one day. Pre-Black Friday (announcement to November 21), Black Friday week (November 22–29), and post-sale (what is still available, what sold out) are three content phases. Each one brings its own searches.

Link to your evergreen content. Deal readers should find your game guides, hardware explainers, and network comparisons one click away. Our guide to the best ad networks for gaming blogs explains which networks pay best for gaming traffic year-round.

Nintendo's Black Friday 2026 lineup — a $499.99 Switch 2 bundle, $20 off rare first-party discounts, and an eShop sale starting November 19 — is the biggest gaming deal event of the holiday season.
