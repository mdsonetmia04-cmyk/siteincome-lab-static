Black Friday 2026 falls on Friday, November 27, and the biggest discounts of the year are not on televisions or sneakers this time. They are on the AI tools that bloggers, creators, and small publishers use every single day to write, rank, and earn. Dozens of AI writing assistants, SEO platforms, and voice tools are cutting 30 to 50 percent off their annual plans, and the smartest blogs are already turning those deal searches into ad revenue.

This guide covers the verified Black Friday 2026 AI tool deals that are live right now, the exact prices deal trackers have confirmed on the vendors' own pages, which tools are genuinely worth buying, and how a blog can turn a week of deal hunting into months of traffic and income. Why AI Tools Own Black Friday 2026

A year ago, AI tools were a curiosity for most bloggers. Today they are the workflow. The average independent publisher now leans on an AI writer for first drafts, an AI SEO tool for briefs and optimization, and often an AI voice tool for video or podcast versions of the same article. That shift is why Black Friday 2026 looks different from every sale before it: the tools on offer are not nice-to-have extras, they are the production line itself.

Trackers are following 79 AI tools with confirmed or expected discounts this year, and the pattern is consistent: writing and video tools are discounting 30 to 50 percent off annual plans, a handful bundle bonus credits or extra months, and all-in-one platforms run their deepest discount of the year. Most offers run from mid-November through Cyber Monday on November 30, and the best prices require paying for a full year up front.

There is a second reason this category matters for publishers. Readers searching for a specific tool deal are close to buying, so they stay longer, click more, and convert better on ads and affiliate links than casual readers. A single well-timed deals post can earn more in one week than a month of ordinary articles.

AI Writing Tools: The Biggest Discounts of the Week

Writing tools are the most consistently discounted category in this year's sale. There are more AI writers competing for the same bloggers than ever before, and Black Friday is when they fight hardest for annual subscribers.

Koala AI: 40% Off Annual Plans

Koala AI is the deal most bloggers will actually buy. The AI article writer is built specifically for SEO-optimized blog posts, using real-time search analysis to shape each article around what is already ranking. Multiple trackers confirm 40 percent off annual plans, November 13 to December 2, with Starter at $15 a month billed annually ($180 a year), down from $25 a month. One tracker also lists a 35 percent variant with code KOALABF35, so take whichever is better when you land on the page. Koala is the tool most small publishers use to produce the volume ad revenue depends on. If you buy one writing tool this Black Friday, start here.

Jasper AI: 20% Off Annual Plans Plus 150,000 Bonus Words

Jasper AI is the veteran of this category, a long-form AI writing assistant built for marketing teams and content agencies, with a Canvas workspace for campaign planning. Its 2026 offer is 20 percent off annual plans plus 150,000 bonus words, with Pro at $59 a month billed annually ($708 a year), November 15 to December 4. Past cycles saw discounts closer to 40 percent off, so this year's offer is smaller in percentage terms but richer in bonus words. Jasper suits publishers running a content operation, not a solo blog: agencies and teams needing brand voice controls across writers. Buy Jasper if you manage content for others; buy Koala if you write for yourself.

Writesonic: 33% Off Annual Plans

Writesonic is offering 33 percent off all annual subscriptions, with the Individual plan at $13.33 a month billed annually at $160 a year, using the code BFCM33, running from November 10 to December 5. It covers blog posts, ads, product descriptions, and social content in one subscription, suiting bloggers who also sell products or run a store. It sits in the middle: less specialized than Koala, less team-oriented than Jasper, but cheaper than both and broader than either. At $13.33 a month it is one of the lowest-priced annual writing subscriptions in the sale.

Rytr: 30% Off the Annual Unlimited Plan

Rytr is the cheapest way into an AI writer this Black Friday. Its Unlimited annual plan is 30 percent off at $6.30 a month billed annually ($75.60 a year), code RYTRBF30, November 12 to December 2. Rytr suits new bloggers needing short-form help: captions, newsletters, blurbs, and outlines. It will not produce a publish-ready 3,000-word article alone, but at $6.30 a month it is almost impossible to argue with as a first AI writer.

Frase: 30% Off Annual Plans

Frase is an SEO content tool that starts from the search results and works backward, building briefs and drafts around the questions and headings that already rank. Its offer is 30 percent off annual subscriptions, with Solo at $10.50 a month billed annually ($126 a year), code FRASEBF30, November 12 to December 5. Frase is the research step before writing: feed it a keyword and it returns the questions people ask, the headings competitors use, and the gaps your article can fill. Paired with Koala, it covers the full workflow from research to draft.

AI SEO Tools: Where the Real Money Is Saved

Writing tools get the headlines, but SEO tools save bloggers the most money, because these are the priciest subscriptions a publisher pays for. A single all-in-one platform can cost $130 a month or more, so even 20 percent off is worth hundreds a year.

Scalenut: 60% Off Annual Plans

Scalenut is the headline SEO deal of Black Friday 2026. The AI SEO platform, which combines keyword clustering with a content optimizer, is 60 percent off annual plans with double the usual limits, and Starter is $24 a month billed annually, down from $59 a month. One tracker reports discounts up to 70 percent off on the higher Max annual plans, with triple the usual AI limits. Scalenut replaces two subscriptions at once: keyword research and content optimization. For a publisher building topical authority, that is the core workflow, and 60 percent off undercuts many single-purpose tools. If you can afford only one SEO platform this year, this is the strongest deal in the sale.

NeuronWriter: 50% Off Yearly, or 88% Off Lifetime

NeuronWriter is the deal hunter's favorite in this category. The AI SEO writer is 50 percent off yearly plans, and through AppSumo it is also available as a lifetime deal at 88 percent off, from $89 one time against an equivalent $750 a year. That is the single best value in the entire sale for a blogger who plans to still be publishing in two or three years, because the subscription never renews. Only buy a lifetime deal for a tool you have tried: run a real article through the trial this week, then decide. If it fits, $89 once beats a year of almost any competing SEO writer.

SE Ranking: Extra 20% Off Annual Plans

SE Ranking is the all-in-one platform most small publishers graduate to when they outgrow free tools. Its offer is an extra 20 percent off annual plans for new customers, live until December 4, with Core at $82.56 a month on the sale price against a normal $103.20 a month on yearly billing ($129 monthly). It covers rank tracking, site audits, backlink monitoring, and AI search tracking in one dashboard. Twenty percent sounds modest next to Scalenut's 60, but the tools do different jobs: SE Ranking is a rank tracker and auditor first, a content tool second; Scalenut is the reverse. If your blog already ranks and you need to defend positions, SE Ranking is the right purchase.

Rank Math PRO: Up to 40% Off Yearly Plans

Rank Math is the WordPress SEO plugin most bloggers install on day one, and PRO is the single most important Black Friday purchase for any WordPress blog. Trackers report up to 40 percent off yearly plans, with PRO from $4.99 a month billed yearly against a regular $8.99, and one lists up to 50 percent off with a countdown to November 23, so the deepest cut may expire before the rest of the sale. PRO adds advanced schema, rank tracking inside WordPress, and analytics showing which posts are winning and slipping. At roughly $5 a month it is the cheapest serious SEO purchase here, and it improves every post you have already published. If you run WordPress and buy nothing else, buy Rank Math PRO before November 23.

AlsoAsked, Plerdy, Citation Rocket, and Promptwatch

Four smaller tools round out the SEO deals, each solving a specific problem the big platforms handle badly. AlsoAsked, a People Also Ask research tool, is 40 percent off annual plans at $9 a month billed $108 yearly, ending November 30. Plerdy, which combines conversion optimization and UX tools with an SEO checker, is 50 percent off yearly plans for the first 12 months, with Startup at $126 for the first year, code plerdyBF2026, ending December 2. Citation Rocket audits your site's readiness for AI search across five AI engines and 13 AI crawlers, returning ranked fixes. It is 40 percent off lifetime licences at $59 one time, down from $99, ending December 2. Promptwatch tracks your brand's visibility across ChatGPT, AI Overviews, AI Mode, Copilot, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Meta AI, and DeepSeek, and it is 30 percent off the first year of an annual plan at $798 on the Essential plan, ending November 30. That is professional pricing, but as traffic increasingly comes from AI answers, knowing where you appear matters as much as knowing where you rank.

Surfer SEO, Mangools, Ahrefs, and Semrush: The Ones Not on Sale

Not every tool discounts. Surfer SEO, which scores drafts against top-ranking competitors, has announced nothing for Black Friday 2026 as of November 21; its last cycle ran November 27 to December 5 without disclosing the discount, so a late announcement is possible, but there is nothing to buy today. Mangools has teased a deal without naming a price or date; its 2025 cycle offered 35 percent off yearly plans. Ahrefs never discounts, by policy: Lite is $129 a month, or $107.50 billed yearly, with a $29 Starter plan as the cheaper way in. Semrush has announced no sale and ran no official one last year either; its SEO plan is $139.95 a month, or $117.33 billed yearly, with a free plan and a 7-day trial. Do not plan your budget around either tool. Buy the tools that are actually discounting, and use the free tiers of Ahrefs and Semrush for the research only they can do.

Blogging is no longer just text: the same article that earns ad revenue on your site can earn again as a narrated video or podcast episode. ElevenLabs is the AI voice generator most creators use to turn articles into audio, and its offer is 30 percent off the annual Creator plan at $15.40 a month billed annually ($184.80 a year), code LISTENMORE30, November 10 to December 5. Hostinger's AI website builder is also up to 80 percent off with double AI credits on longer plans, the budget option for a brand-new site built from scratch.

How Bloggers Turn AI Deal Searches Into Ad Revenue

A deals post is a traffic asset that pays in three ways at once. The first is display advertising. Readers searching for a specific AI tool deal arrive with commercial intent, and advertisers pay more to reach people about to spend money than casual browsers. Deal content earns higher rates per thousand visitors than general content, and the gap is largest during Black Friday week itself. Publish early so the post is indexed and ranking when search volume peaks. For a deeper look at how display earnings actually work, see our guide on how much Adsterra pays per 1,000 visitors, which breaks down real CPM rates by country. The second way is affiliate revenue: most tools here run affiliate programs, and a reader who buys an annual plan through your link can earn you a commission worth far more than the ad revenue from that visit. Disclose the relationship clearly, as United States law requires, and recommend only tools you would genuinely use. The third way is the post itself: a guide naming real prices keeps attracting searches for months. Update the prices when the sale ends and keep the page live. Our earlier guides to Black Friday VPN deals and hosting deals showed the same pattern: blogs that published two weeks early captured the search wave, and the ones that waited fought for scraps.

The First-Year Trap: What to Check Before You Buy

Almost every discount here covers the first term only: a tool at $15 a month on its Black Friday plan may renew at $25 a month in year two. Before you buy, find the renewal price, check the discount applies to the plan you need, and set a reminder for eleven months out so you can cancel or renegotiate before the full price hits. Never buy an annual plan for a tool you have not tried. Every tool here offers a free plan or a trial, and a week of real use tells you more than any review, including this one. A $15 a month subscription to a tool you never open is not a bargain. It is a leak.

Which Deals Are Actually Worth It?

Starting out on under $20 a month? Buy Koala AI at $15 a month for writing and Rank Math PRO at roughly $5 a month for on-page SEO. That pair covers the full workflow from draft to ranking, and it is the highest-value combination in the sale. If your blog already earns, add Scalenut at $24 a month for keyword clusters and optimization, and consider NeuronWriter's $89 lifetime deal. ElevenLabs at $15.40 a month turns every post into a second piece of content if you publish audio or video versions. For a team or agency, Jasper at $59 a month is the professional choice, and SE Ranking at $82.56 a month gives you the rank tracking client work requires. Skip Ahrefs and Semrush this week: neither is discounting, and both cost the same in December. Frequently Asked Questions

When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday 2026 falls on Friday, November 27, and Cyber Monday follows on Monday, November 30. Most AI tool discounts started in mid-November and run through Cyber Monday. The earliest end dates in this guide are November 23 for Rank Math's deepest cut and November 30 for AlsoAsked and Promptwatch.

Is it better to buy an annual plan or a lifetime deal?

An annual plan suits a tool you are trying for the first time, because you can walk away after a year. A lifetime deal suits a tool you already know and will use for years, because it never renews. NeuronWriter's $89 deal is the standout lifetime offer this year, but only buy it after a trial run.

Can a small blog actually earn from Black Friday deal content?

Yes. Deal content is one of the highest-earning categories for small blogs, because deal searchers are close to buying: they stay longer, click more, and earn higher ad rates. Publish early, name real prices and end dates, and keep the page updated after the sale.
