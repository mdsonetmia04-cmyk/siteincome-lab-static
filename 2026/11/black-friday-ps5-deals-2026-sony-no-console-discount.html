Black Friday PS5 Deals 2026: No Console Discount — DualSense $54.99, PS VR2 $100 Off and PS Plus Premium $107.19 — How Gaming Blogs Turn the Year's Biggest Console Sale Into Ad Revenue

Sony cut exactly $0 off the PlayStation 5 this Black Friday. Not the disc console at $649.99, not the Digital Edition at $599.99, and not the PS5 Pro at $899.99 — every console is full price, and that is the single most important thing to know before you shop. But before you close the tab, here is the twist that makes this sale worth your time anyway: the DualSense controller is $54.99, down $20 from $74.99, at Amazon, Best Buy, Target and PlayStation Direct; the PlayStation VR2 is $100 off; the Pulse Elite headset is $109 instead of $149; and a full year of PlayStation Plus Premium costs $107.19, a third off its $159.99 list price. The console did not go on sale. Almost everything you plug into it did.

Black Friday 2026 falls on Friday, November 27, with Cyber Monday following on November 30. Sony's own PlayStation Black Friday sale went live on November 16 and runs through November 30, and most of the deals in this guide are already live. Amazon's Black Friday Week runs November 19 through November 30, Best Buy's sale runs through November 28, and Target's event runs November 22 through November 29. Every price here was checked against deal coverage published on November 24 and 25, 2026 — always confirm the price at checkout before you buy.

The Headline Nobody Expected: No PS5 Console Discount in 2026

Let us start with the bad news, because it shapes every decision in this guide. Sony's Black Friday 2026 sale, announced on the PlayStation Blog and updated on November 15, includes discounts on controllers, headsets, the PlayStation VR2, games and PlayStation Plus memberships — and it never mentions a PS5 console. Deal trackers who read every item on PlayStation Direct's Black Friday page on November 23 confirmed the same thing: no console had a sale price. The PS5 is $649.99, the Digital Edition is $599.99, and the PS5 Pro is $899.99, and those are the prices you will pay on Black Friday itself.

That is a sharp reversal from last year, when Sony cut every PS5 model by $100 — which is why so many shoppers expected a similar deal this time. The reason there is no repeat is the price rise Sony applied on April 2, 2026, which pushed every model up by $100 or more. The console that cost $549.99 in early 2026 now costs $649.99, and Sony is not about to discount a console it has just made more expensive.

The PS5 Pro is the hardest hit: at $899.99 it rose by $150 in April, and there is no Black Friday discount on it either. The cheapest official route is Sony's Certified Refurbished Pro at $849 — $50 under the new price, same 2TB SSD, 12-month warranty — but stock is limited, and on November 23 neither the new Pro nor the refurbished one could actually be bought on PlayStation Direct. If you want a Pro this week, a retailer bundle or waiting are your realistic options.

None of this means the sale is a dud — it means the sale is different, and different is what makes it interesting for buyers and bloggers alike. A console that never goes on sale creates a different kind of search traffic: people stop searching "PS5 Black Friday deal" and start searching "is the PS5 on sale", "cheapest way to buy PS5" and "PS5 bundle deals". Those are high-intent searches from people who have already decided to buy. The money this Black Friday is not in the console. It is in everything around it.

DualSense Controllers: The Best PS5 Deal of Black Friday 2026

If you buy one thing in this sale, make it a second controller. The standard DualSense wireless controller is $54.99 at Amazon, Best Buy, Target and PlayStation Direct, down from $74.99 — a $20 saving, or 27% off. The deal covers a wide range of colours, including Midnight Black, White, Cosmic Red, Galactic Purple, Gray Camouflage and Starlight Blue, all at $54.99. The premium colourways are discounted too: the Chroma finishes, including Chroma Pearl, Chroma Indigo and Chroma Teal, are $59.99 instead of $79.99, and the ombre colours are $64.99 instead of $84.99. The offer runs through December 7, 2026, or while promotional pricing and supplies last.

That $54.99 price is worth pausing on: it ties last year's Black Friday low, beats the previous Amazon low of $59 from June 2026 by $4, and is the lowest price Amazon has shown for the controller in 90 days. At $74.99, the DualSense is a pricey spare; at $54.99, it is the cheapest way to turn a solo night on the PS5 into couch co-op, and the natural second purchase for anyone who just bought a console for GTA 6. It is also the gift that fits almost every PS5 owner on your list — which is why controllers sell out faster than any other accessory in this sale.

The pro-grade DualSense Edge is $169.99, down from $199.99 — a $30 saving on the controller with replaceable stick modules, back buttons and a carrying case. The Edge deal ends December 14, a week later than the standard DualSense offer, so there is slightly less urgency — but Edge stock has always been thinner, and the Midnight Black version tends to go first.

Beyond the discount, you are paying for the hardware itself: the DualSense's haptic feedback and adaptive triggers are still the most distinctive thing about playing on a PS5, and they are why a second controller feels like an upgrade rather than a duplicate. With a partner, a sibling or a child, it doubles the value of every game you own; alone, it is the spare that keeps you playing when the first controller's battery dies mid-session. At $54.99, it is the easiest yes in this entire sale.

PlayStation VR2 Is $100 Off — The Cheapest Way Into PlayStation VR

The second-biggest hardware saving is the PlayStation VR2, $100 off at PlayStation Direct and participating retailers. It turns a PS5 into a full virtual-reality console, with exclusives such as Horizon Call of the Mountain and VR modes for Gran Turismo 7 and No Man's Sky. In the UK, the headline bundle tells the same story: the VR2 with Horizon Call of the Mountain is £309.99, down from £399.99. Some VR2 items leave the sale before November 30, so do not wait for the weekend to decide.

VR2 has always been a harder sell than a controller — it is an accessory for an accessory, and you need VR games to go with it. But at $100 off, in the same week millions of new players are buying PS5s to play GTA 6, the maths changes. A VR headset is also the most giftable item in this sale: a complete experience in one box, visibly different from anything the recipient already owns, and the one PlayStation product that still feels like the future. If you have been curious about VR for two years, this is the cheapest entry point Sony has offered.

Pulse Elite and Pulse Explore: The Audio Deals Worth Your Money

Sony's audio hardware is discounted across the board, and the savings are straightforward. The Pulse Elite wireless headset is $109, down from $149 — a $40 saving. The Pulse Explore wireless earbuds are $119, down from $149 — a $30 saving. The Access controller, designed for players with disabilities, is $30 off. And the 27-inch PlayStation gaming monitor is $50 off. All of these offers run until December 21, the longest window in the sale, which means there is no rush — but also no reason to wait, because the prices will not get better.

The Pulse Elite is the pick of the audio deals: at $109, it is the official headset built for the PS5's 3D audio, and $40 off puts it below most third-party headsets with the same feature set. The Pulse Explore earbuds, at $119, are the portable option for PlayStation Portal players. Neither will sell out the way the DualSense will, but both are genuine savings on hardware that rarely goes on sale. If audio is on your list, buy the Elite now and stop thinking about it.

PlayStation Plus Black Friday: Premium for $107.19 — But Read the Catch

The PlayStation Plus deal is the best value in Sony's entire sale and the one with the biggest catch, so read this section carefully. For Black Friday 2026, Sony has cut all three 12-month PlayStation Plus tiers: Essential is $63.99 instead of $79.99, a 20% saving of $16; Extra is $101.24 instead of $134.99, a 25% saving of $33.75; and Premium is $107.19 instead of $159.99, a 33% saving of $52.80. The offer runs from November 16 through November 30, and it applies to 12-month plans only — one-month and three-month plans are not discounted.

Here is the catch: these prices are for players who join PlayStation Plus during the promotion. If you are already a member, you cannot renew your current tier at the sale price. Current Premium members get nothing at all; current Essential and Extra members get exactly one door left open — a discounted upgrade to Premium. So if you already pay for Essential, this sale does not make your renewal cheaper. It makes upgrading to Premium cheaper, a deliberate choice by Sony to push players up the tiers.

Even with the catch, Premium is the story of this sale: it costs just $5.95 more than Extra for the entire year — $0.50 per month — and for that you get everything in Extra plus the Classics Catalog, game trials and cloud streaming. If you are joining for the first time, or returning after a break, Premium at $107.19 is the obvious choice. One warning for every tier: the sale price covers year one only. After 12 months, your membership renews automatically at full price — $159.99 for Premium, $134.99 for Extra, $79.99 for Essential — unless you turn off auto-renew. Set a reminder now, or the saving evaporates in year two.

PlayStation Store Sale: Up to 75% Off Games

The PlayStation Store Black Friday sale covers hundreds of PS5 and PS4 titles, with discounts of up to 75% on select games, and it runs through November 30. A second wave is live now, adding Madden 27, NBA 2K27 and Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced through 11:59 PM PT on November 30. Standout prices reported this week: Ghost of Yotei is $34.99, down from $69.99; Marvel's Wolverine is $49.99, down from $69.99; and the NBA 2K27 Deluxe Edition is $44.99, down from $99.99. Digital games are the one category where the discounts genuinely match the Black Friday hype — and the category where the best prices disappear first, so check the store twice if you missed something the first time.

The GTA 6 Effect: Why PlayStation Deals Matter More This Year

None of these deals exists in a vacuum. GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026 — eight days before Black Friday — and it is the biggest game launch in history. As we covered in our earlier GTA 6 launch report, the game earned $2.36 billion in a single day with a 99 Metacritic score. Millions of players are buying consoles, controllers and headsets this week specifically to play GTA 6, and every one of them is searching for deals at the same time. A controller at $54.99 is not just a discount this year — it is the second controller for the biggest game of the decade.

Retailer Bundles: Where to Find a PS5 Below List Price Anyway

Sony may not discount the console, but retailers can build their own bundles — and one beats the list price. Walmart is selling a PS5 Slim Digital Console bundle with an extra red DualSense controller and an accessories kit for $699.99, down from $749.99. The console alone is $599.99, so the bundle is effectively a controller and accessories for $100 — the closest thing to a console discount available in the US this week. Walmart also has the PlayStation Portal with an accessories kit for $294.99, down from $329.99; the Portal alone is $249.99, so the bundle is for buyers who want the kit.

Outside the PlayStation ecosystem, the rival VR deal is worth knowing: Walmart has the Meta Quest 3S 128GB for $297, down from $349.99, with a $50 gift card included — a genuine alternative if the person on your list wants VR but does not own a PS5. The Quest 3S plays standalone and needs no console. It is the one deal here that competes with Sony rather than complementing it, and for a gift buyer not locked into PlayStation, it is the better buy.

What to Buy, What to Skip

Buy the DualSense at $54.99 without thinking — it is the best price of the year on the accessory every PS5 owner needs. Buy PlayStation Plus Premium at $107.19 if you are joining for the first time, because $5.95 over Extra for a whole year is the best value in the sale. Buy the PS VR2 at $100 off if you have wanted VR for a year and own a PS5 already. Buy the games you actually want to play this month, because digital discounts of 50 to 75% are the deepest cuts in the sale. Skip the PS5 Pro at $899.99 unless you need it this week — there is no discount, and a refurbished Pro at $849 is only worth it if you can catch one in stock. And skip any "deal" that is just a console at list price with a full-price game added: add up the parts before you call it a bundle.

How Gaming Blogs Turn PS5 Deal Season Into Ad Revenue

A week like this one is the most profitable week of the year for a gaming blog, and the reason is search intent. "PS5 Black Friday deals" is searched by people with their card in hand; "is the PS5 on sale" is searched by people who have decided to buy and need one final push. A post that answers those searches honestly — real prices, real end dates, real catches — earns the click, keeps the reader on the page, and converts that attention into ad impressions. The format that works is the one in this post: a deal table at the top, one section per deal, and a clear buy-or-skip verdict. Update the prices daily while the sale runs, because a stale price is the fastest way to lose a reader's trust.
