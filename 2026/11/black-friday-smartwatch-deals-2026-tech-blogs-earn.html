Apple Watch Deals: Series 12, Series 11, SE 3, Ultra 3 and Ultra 4

Apple dominates Black Friday smartwatch searches every year, and 2026 is no exception. This year there is a genuinely good Apple Watch at every price from $179 to $700.

Apple Watch Series 12: $299 at Amazon

The Apple Watch Series 12 is the newest mainstream Apple Watch, and it is already $100 off. Amazon has the 42mm GPS model for $299, down from the $399 list price, and the 46mm GPS model for $349, down from $449. You get the bright always-on display, the health sensors, the fitness tracking, and the deep iPhone integration no other watch can match. The Series 12 is the right pick for most iPhone owners because it is the newest watch with the longest software support ahead of it.

Apple Watch Series 11: $329 at Walmart

If the Series 12 is the headline, the Series 11 is the quiet bargain. Walmart has the 46mm GPS Apple Watch Series 11 for $329, down from $429 — the same $100 saving, on a watch one generation older and still excellent. For most people the day-to-day difference between the two is small: the same core health and fitness features, the same app ecosystem, the same iPhone integration. The Series 11 makes sense if you want the larger 46mm case for $20 less, or if you are buying a gift.

Apple Watch SE 3: $179 at Amazon

The Apple Watch SE 3 is the deal of the entire smartwatch category this year. Amazon has the 40mm GPS model for $179, down from the $249 list price — a $70 cut that takes it below $200 for the first time and sets a new record low. At $179 it is genuinely cheap for what it is: a real Apple Watch with the health sensors, the fitness tracking, the notifications, and the safety features, minus the always-on display and a few of the most advanced sensors. This is the watch to buy for a first-time smartwatch owner, for a kid or a teenager, or for someone who wants an Apple Watch mainly for notifications and workouts. It is the deal most likely to sell out before Friday.

Apple Watch Ultra 3: $649 on Amazon

The Apple Watch Ultra 3 has dropped to $649 on Amazon, down from $799 — a $150 saving on Apple's rugged flagship. The Ultra line is built for people who run, hike, swim, and dive in conditions that would destroy a normal watch: a tougher case, a brighter display, a longer battery, and a depth gauge and dual-frequency GPS that the standard models do not have. At $649 it is still expensive, but it is $150 cheaper than a month ago, and Ultra models almost never see discounts this deep. If you want the biggest battery and the brightest screen Apple makes, this is the price to buy at.

Apple Watch Ultra 4: $699.99 on Amazon

The brand-new Apple Watch Ultra 4 is already on sale. Amazon has it for $699.99, down from the $799 list price — a $100 cut on a watch that has barely been out. Flagship watches in their first months almost never get discounted, and when they do, the discount is usually small and short-lived. The Ultra 4 is for the buyer who wants the newest thing and is willing to pay for it, but still wants to feel clever about the price. At $699.99 you get the latest rugged Apple Watch for $50 more than the discounted Ultra 3.

Samsung Galaxy Watch Deals: Watch 8, Watch 8 Classic and Watch Ultra

Samsung's watches are the best smartwatches for Android owners, and the Black Friday discounts are deep — especially on the premium models. The Galaxy Watch line pairs with any Android phone, works best with a Samsung Galaxy phone, and offers the most complete health and fitness tracking outside of Apple's ecosystem.

Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra: $465.55 on Amazon

The Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra is the single biggest dollar saving in this guide: $465.55 on Amazon, down from $649.99 — a cut of more than $184. It is Samsung's answer to the Apple Watch Ultra: a chunky titanium case, a bright display, a long battery, and the most complete set of health sensors Samsung has ever put in a watch. At $465.55 it undercuts the discounted Apple Watch Ultra 3 by nearly $185 while offering a comparable feature set for Android users. If you own a Samsung phone, this is the deal to grab.

Samsung Galaxy Watch 8 Classic: $349.99 on Amazon

The Galaxy Watch 8 Classic is the watch for people who miss the rotating bezel. Amazon has the 46mm Bluetooth model for $349.99, down from $499.99 — a $150 saving on the model with the physical rotating bezel that makes scrolling through notifications feel satisfying in a way a touchscreen never will. The Classic is the right pick if you want a watch that looks like a watch rather than a miniature phone on your wrist. It has the same health and fitness features as the standard Watch 8, plus a more traditional design.

Samsung Galaxy Watch 8: around $270 at Woot

The standard Galaxy Watch 8 is the mainstream Samsung pick, and Woot has the 40mm GPS model in Graphite for just south of $270 — about $30 off the $299.99 list price. That is a smaller discount than the premium models, but it is still a genuine saving on the current-generation watch, and the Watch 8 is the model most Android owners should buy: light, fast, with the full set of Samsung Health features and a battery that comfortably lasts a day and a half. The $270 price puts it in the same territory as the Apple Watch SE 3.

Google Pixel Watch 5: $299.99 on Amazon

The Google Pixel Watch 5 is at $299.99 on Amazon, down from $399.99 — a clean $100 saving on Google's newest watch. The Pixel Watch 5 is the best smartwatch for Google households: it runs Wear OS with the deepest Gemini integration of any watch, it pairs naturally with a Pixel phone, and it brings Fitbit's health and fitness tracking into Google's ecosystem. The battery life is better than the previous generation, the display is brighter, and the AI features are the most advanced of any smartwatch in this guide. If you own a Pixel phone, or you live in Google's apps, the Pixel Watch 5 at $299.99 is the obvious choice. It works with any Android phone, but not with an iPhone.

Garmin Deals: Instinct 2X, Forerunner 265 and vivoactive 5

Garmin is the brand for people who train seriously. Its watches are not the prettiest and they are not the smartest, but they have the best GPS accuracy, the longest battery life, and the most detailed training data of any watch you can buy. Black Friday is the one week of the year when Garmin's premium models get real discounts.

Garmin Instinct 2X Solar Tactical: $319.99 on Amazon

The Garmin Instinct 2X Solar Tactical is down to $319.99 on Amazon, from $499.99 — a $180 saving on a watch that almost never goes on sale. The Instinct line is Garmin's rugged series: a solar-charging display, a battery that lasts weeks rather than days, and a design built to survive conditions that would kill a normal smartwatch. The Tactical edition adds night-vision compatibility and a stealth mode that turns off wireless communication. This is the watch for hikers, soldiers, hunters, and anyone who spends days away from a charger. The solar charging means the battery effectively never dies in daylight.

Garmin Forerunner 265: $299.99 on Amazon

The Garmin Forerunner 265 is the runner's watch, and it is $299.99 on Amazon, down from $449.99 — a $150 saving. The Forerunner 265 has the bright AMOLED display that the older Forerunners lacked, plus Garmin's full training suite: training readiness scores, race predictions, recovery advice, and detailed running dynamics that no Apple or Samsung watch can match. If you run regularly — three or more times a week, training for a race, tracking your pace and your heart rate — the Forerunner 265 is the best watch in this guide for your money. The $150 discount makes it cheaper than the Apple Watch Series 12 while offering far more training data.

Garmin vivoactive 5: $168 on Amazon

The Garmin vivoactive 5 is the budget Garmin, and it is the best value in the entire Garmin range this Black Friday: $168 on Amazon, down from $299.99 — a saving of about $132. The vivoactive 5 is the watch for people who want Garmin's health and fitness tracking without the rugged looks or the premium price: a bright AMOLED display, up to 11 days of battery life, and the same sleep, stress, and body-battery tracking as the more expensive models. At $168, the vivoactive 5 costs less than the Apple Watch SE 3 and lasts five times longer on a charge. That battery life means it tracks your sleep every single night.

Best Budget Smartwatch Deals Under $200

Not everyone needs a $700 watch. The budget end of Black Friday is where the smartest shopping happens, because a $59 watch that does 80 percent of what a $400 watch does is a better buy for most people than the $400 watch itself. The Apple Watch SE 3 at $179 is the best budget pick for iPhone owners, and the Garmin vivoactive 5 at $168 is the best budget pick for everyone else. Below them sits the CMF Watch 3 Pro at $59 — the lowest price it has ever reached. CMF is the budget brand from Nothing, and the Watch 3 Pro offers a bright AMOLED display, Bluetooth calling, and solid fitness tracking for less than the cost of a dinner out. Decide what you actually need, and buy the cheapest watch that does those things well.

Which Smartwatch Should You Buy?

The first question is not which watch is best. It is which phone you own. An Apple Watch only works with an iPhone. A Galaxy Watch and a Pixel Watch only work with Android phones. Garmin, Fitbit, and CMF work with both. Buy the wrong watch for your phone and you own an expensive bracelet, no matter how good the deal was. If you own an iPhone, the choice is straightforward: the Apple Watch SE 3 at $179 if you want value, the Series 12 at $299 if you want the best mainstream watch, and the Ultra 3 at $649 or the Ultra 4 at $699.99 if you want the rugged flagship. If you own a Samsung phone, the Galaxy Watch Ultra at $465.55 is the best deal in this guide, and the Galaxy Watch 8 at around $270 is the best value. If you own a Pixel phone, the Pixel Watch 5 at $299.99 is the natural match. If you train seriously, buy a Garmin regardless of your phone.

How Tech Blogs Turn Smartwatch Deal Traffic Into Ad Revenue

Here is the part most readers never think about: every one of these deals is also a business opportunity for the blogs that publish them. A single well-ranked Black Friday smartwatch post can earn more ad revenue in one week than a regular blog post earns in a year, and the reason is traffic volume, buyer intent, and advertising economics.

It starts with search volume. In the week before Black Friday, searches for terms like "Black Friday smartwatch deals," "Apple Watch Black Friday," and "Garmin Black Friday deal" spike by hundreds of percent. Millions of people type the same questions into Google at the same time, and the blogs that rank for those questions get the traffic. Then there is intent: a reader searching for a smartwatch deal has their wallet open and is comparing prices. That makes them far more valuable to advertisers than a reader who arrived from a casual social media scroll. During Black Friday week, the effective CPM — the amount an advertiser pays per thousand ad views — on a deal post can be several times higher than on an ordinary article, because every impression is a shopper.

But the traffic does not arrive by accident. The blogs that earn the most from deal season publish early, so Google has time to index them before the search spike arrives; update daily, because prices change hourly and Google rewards fresh content; cover the whole category rather than a single product; and promote the post everywhere — social media, newsletters, and internal links from every related article. There is also a second revenue stream: affiliate and referral income. Ad networks like Adsterra add a stream through their referral programs — publishers who refer other publishers earn a percentage of the referred publisher's revenue for life. That is how a single Black Friday post can keep earning long after the sale ends. Deal season is not just a traffic spike; it is the highest-leverage week of the year for a monetized blog.

7 Mistakes to Avoid When Buying a Smartwatch on Black Friday

First, buying the wrong watch for your phone. An Apple Watch will not pair with an Android phone, and a Galaxy Watch will not pair with an iPhone — check compatibility before you check the price. Second, trusting the "was" price: check the list price against the manufacturer's own site. Third, buying the wrong size — measure your wrist first. Fourth, ignoring the return policy: buy from a retailer with a proper return policy and keep the box until you are sure. Fifth, forgetting the extras: a charger, a second band, a screen protector. Sixth, paying full price for last year's hardware. Seventh, waiting too long: the Apple Watch SE 3 at $179 and the Galaxy Watch Ultra at $465.55 are the two most likely to disappear before Friday. If you have decided which watch you want, buy it now.
