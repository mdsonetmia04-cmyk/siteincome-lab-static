Black Friday 2026 falls on Friday, November 27, with Cyber Monday following on November 30 and Thanksgiving on November 26. But the Switch 2 deals did not wait for the calendar. Nintendo's official Black Friday offers started on November 22, Woot's $469 doorbuster is live now and runs through November 30 or until stock sells out, and Best Buy kicked off a 10-day Black Friday sale on November 19. If you want a Switch 2 under the tree this year, the window is already open — and, as every gaming blog covering this sale has learned, the best deals on a Nintendo console do not last until Friday.

Why Switch 2 Discounts Are So Rare — and Why $30 Off Matters

To understand why a $30 discount on the Switch 2 is front-page news, you have to understand how Nintendo prices its hardware. The Switch 2 launched worldwide on June 5, 2025, at $449.99 for the standalone console and $499.99 for the bundle with Mario Kart World. It sold more than 3.5 million units in its first four days, the fastest-selling Nintendo hardware ever, and by September 30, 2026, Nintendo reported 26.35 million units sold. The original Switch sold around 150 million units across its eight-year run, making it one of the best-selling consoles of all time — and the Switch 2 is tracking ahead of it.

Then, on September 1, 2026, Nintendo raised the price. The standalone console went from $449.99 to $499.99 in the United States, and from 469.99 euros to 499.99 euros in Europe. The company announced the increase in May 2026, citing market conditions, and it landed just as the holiday season approached. That $50 hike is why the Woot deal matters so much: $469 does not just save you $30 off today's price, it brings the console back to within $20 of its original launch price.

Nintendo consoles rarely go on sale for a simple reason: they do not need to. Demand has outstripped supply for most of the Switch 2's life, and Nintendo has historically protected its hardware pricing while discounting software instead. The official Nintendo Black Friday deals for 2026 follow exactly that pattern — the console itself is not discounted by Nintendo at all. The $50 saving on the Mario Kart World bundle and the $20 off select physical games from November 22 to 29 are Nintendo's official offers, and the digital game sale runs from November 19 to December 2. The $469 standalone price is a retailer move by Woot, not a Nintendo price cut, which is why it is limited to one per customer and only while supplies last.

That scarcity is real. Woot deals have a habit of disappearing fast, and this one has already been restocked at least once after selling through.

Nintendo Switch 2 for $469 at Woot — the Headline Deal

The single best Nintendo Switch Black Friday deal live today is the Switch 2 for $469 at Woot, down from its $499.99 list price. Woot is owned by Amazon, the console is brand new in its retail box rather than refurbished, and Amazon Prime members get free shipping. The package includes everything in the standard retail box: the Switch 2 console, the light blue left Joy-Con 2 controller, the light red right Joy-Con 2 controller, the dock, the charging equipment, and the HDMI cable you need to connect it to a television. Woot lists a 90-day limited warranty with the purchase.

There are two catches, and both are worth knowing before you click buy. First, the limit is one console per customer, so you cannot buy one for yourself and one as a gift in the same order. Second, stock is tight. The deal has been live since the early Black Friday sales began, and deal trackers confirmed it was still live on the morning of Tuesday, November 24 — but Woot's own listing stresses that the offer lasts only while inventory lasts. A $30 saving is a 6 percent discount, which sounds small until you remember that this console was $449.99 at launch, rose to $499.99 in September, and has barely budged since. Six percent off a Nintendo console in its first year is genuinely rare.

For comparison, the Switch 2 is holding firm at $499 on Amazon and Best Buy, so the Woot price is $30 cheaper than every major retailer right now. That gap is the whole deal. If you are buying the console on its own — maybe you already own Mario Kart World, or you plan to buy your games separately — the Woot price is the cheapest way to get a brand-new Switch 2 this Black Friday. Just make sure to grab it while supplies last, or before November 30 when the deal expires, if stock is still available.

One more thing to check before you order: the Switch 2 uses microSD Express cards for expandable storage, not the regular microSD cards from the original Switch. The console has 256GB of internal storage, which sounds generous until you install two or three big games.

Switch 2 + Mario Kart World Bundle for $499.99 — the Best Value

Nintendo's official Black Friday deal is the Switch 2 + Mario Kart World bundle for $499.99, down from $549.99, live from November 22 through December 5 at Amazon, Best Buy, and other major retailers. The bundle includes the console, a full digital download of Mario Kart World, and a three-month Nintendo Switch Online membership. At $499.99, you are paying the same price as the standalone console's list price — and getting the biggest game on the system thrown in.

Mario Kart World is not a minor pack-in. It launched alongside the console on June 5, 2025, as an open-world reimagining of the franchise that has been running since 1992, and it sold more than 15 million copies in its first four months. By June 30, 2026, it had sold 15.39 million copies, making it the best-selling game on the system by a wide margin, and by September 30 the figure had climbed to 16.48 million. It is the game most Switch 2 owners buy first, and it is the game that shows off what the new hardware can do — a bigger 7.9-inch screen, up from 6.2 inches on the original Switch, eight times the memory, and a 4K docked output that makes the open world look genuinely stunning on a television.

The maths is simple. Buy the console at Woot for $469 and Mario Kart World separately at $79.99, and you spend $548.99. Buy the bundle for $499.99 and you spend $49 less — and you also get three months of Nintendo Switch Online, which costs $19.99 a year for an individual membership and unlocks online play, the classic games library, and cloud saves. The bundle saves you roughly $50 on the game and the membership combined, on top of the $50 bundle discount itself. Unless you already own Mario Kart World or you actively dislike kart racers, the bundle is the better buy.

There is also a second official bundle: the Switch 2 + Nintendo Switch Sports Resort bundle for $529.99. It follows the same pattern — console plus game, with a $50 saving against buying the two separately — and it is the right choice if your household leans toward motion-controlled party games rather than racing. Sports Resort is the kind of game that gets the whole family playing, and for a console that will live in a shared living room, it can be the smarter pick. Both bundles run through December 5, while stocks last, so you have more time than the Woot doorbuster — but the popular configurations tend to sell out first, and the Mario Kart bundle is the popular one.

Switch 2 Games on Sale This Black Friday

Nintendo's official game deals run from November 22 to 29 for physical games, with the digital sale running longer, from November 19 to December 2. Select physical games are $20 off, and the digital storefront has deeper cuts on individual titles. Here are the game deals worth your attention right now.

Pokémon Legends: Z-A is $49.94 at Amazon, down from $69.99 — a saving of about $20, or 29 percent. It is one of the biggest Switch 2 games of the year, and at under $50 it is the best value game deal in this sale. Sonic X Shadow Generations is $34.99, saving $15. Dragon Quest VII Reimagined is $39.99, saving $20. Yakuza 0 Director's Cut is $32.99, saving $17. NBA 2K27 is $49.99, saving $20.

The Steam Black Friday sale is also worth a look if you play on PC as well as console. Steam opens its Black Friday Discounts and Events hub from November 26 at 10 a.m. Pacific Time through November 30, ahead of its Winter Sale starting on December 17. PC game sales do not directly compete with Switch 2 deals, but many gaming blogs cover both, because the same readers buy on both platforms — and the same deal-hunting traffic that lands on a Switch 2 article often clicks through to a Steam sale roundup too. If you run a gaming blog, that crossover is money.

When you are choosing which games to buy, think about the attach rate. Nintendo reported 73.21 million Switch 2 games sold by September 30, 2026, against 26.35 million consoles — roughly 2.8 games per console. Most new owners buy two or three games with the system, and Black Friday is when those purchases happen. A $20 saving on two games is $40 back in your pocket, which is more than the saving on the console itself at Woot. The games are where the real Black Friday value hides.

Accessories and Storage: the Deal Most Buyers Forget

The most overlooked Black Friday deal in the whole Switch 2 sale is storage. The SanDisk 512GB microSD Express card is $159.99 at Amazon, down from $199.99 — a $40 saving, or 20 percent off. It delivers read speeds up to 880 MB/s and write speeds near 650 MB/s, and it is certified by Nintendo for the Switch 2. That certification matters: the Switch 2 requires microSD Express cards, and a regular microSD card from your old Switch will not work for Switch 2 games. If you buy the console this Black Friday and skip the storage card, you will be back shopping within a week.

The console's 256GB of internal storage fills up fast. A single major Switch 2 title can take up a large chunk of that space, and once you add a couple of games, some screenshots, and a few downloads, you are managing storage instead of playing. The 512GB card effectively triples your usable space, and at $159.99 it is the cheapest it has been.

Beyond storage, the accessory deals follow the usual Black Friday pattern: controllers, cases, and screen protectors are all discounted, and third-party brands are cutting prices more aggressively than Nintendo's own accessories. Dbrand's Black Friday sale offers up to $55 off, plus a free pack of Dbrand triple black playing cards with every order — a small perk, but the kind of detail that deal hunters love. The practical advice is simple: buy the storage card now, buy a case if the console is travelling, and skip the rest until you know which accessories you actually need. A controller is worth it if two people will play together regularly; otherwise, the Joy-Con 2 controllers in the box are enough to start.

Original Switch Deals: the Budget Route Into Nintendo

Not everyone needs a Switch 2, and Black Friday 2026 is also the best time in years to buy the original Switch cheaply. The Switch OLED Mario Kart bundle is $274.99 at major retailers, down from $349.99 — a $75 saving on a console with a gorgeous OLED screen and a full game included.

The renewed deals go further. A renewed Nintendo Switch is $198.99 at Amazon, down from $339 — a cut of roughly 41 percent. A renewed Switch OLED is $265.99, down from $387.99, a cut of about 31 percent. Renewed means the console has been inspected, tested, and cleaned, and it comes with a warranty, but it is not brand new — so check the seller, check the warranty terms, and check the return policy before you buy.

There is a real decision here, and it is worth being honest about. The original Switch plays thousands of games, it has a library built over eight years, and at under $200 renewed it is a fraction of the Switch 2's price. But Nintendo's future is the Switch 2. New releases are increasingly Switch 2 exclusives, and the original Switch is at the end of its life as a platform. If you are buying for a young child who will be happy with Mario Kart 8 and Animal Crossing, the original Switch is the smart buy. If you are buying for yourself or for a teenager who wants the newest games, spend the extra money on the Switch 2. Black Friday makes both choices cheaper — but only one of them is future-proof.

Should You Buy Now or Wait for Black Friday?

Buy now. That is the honest answer for the Switch 2, and it is the opposite of the usual Black Friday advice. The $469 Woot price is live today and ends November 30 or when stock runs out, whichever comes first — and Nintendo has announced no price cut on the console itself, only the bundle deals that are already live. There is no confirmed better price coming on November 27. The Mario Kart World bundle at $499.99 runs through December 5, so that one can wait a few days, but the Woot doorbuster cannot. If the standalone console is the one you want, the risk of waiting is a sold-out listing; the reward for waiting is nothing, because no lower price has been announced anywhere. The same logic applies to the storage card and the game deals — the best prices are already live, and the popular items sell out first. How Gaming Blogs Turn Black Friday Into Ad Revenue

Deal season is the highest-earning week of the year for gaming blogs, and the Switch 2 is a perfect example of why. A single article like this one earns in three ways at once: display ads pay for every reader who lands on the page, affiliate links pay when a reader buys the console or a game, and the surge of search traffic lifts the whole site in Google's rankings for weeks afterwards. The trick is publishing early — the blogs that ranked for Switch 2 deals published their guides before the sales started, while everyone else was still waiting for Friday. If you run a blog, the lesson is simple: cover the deals your readers are already searching for, verify every price, and update the article as prices change.
