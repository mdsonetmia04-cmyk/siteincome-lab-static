GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, and broke nearly every record in entertainment: a 99 on Metacritic, an estimated $2.36 billion in first-day sales, and almost 8 million people watching streams at the same time. Thirteen years after GTA 5, Rockstar Games did not just release a game. It released the biggest launch in the history of games, movies, and music combined.

This post is the launch aftermath, not another preview. We covered the run-up in our GTA 6 launch week guide for gaming blogs. Now the game is out, the reviews are in, and the sales estimates are public. Here is what the review scores say, how much money the game made, what it costs, why streaming records fell, and what it means for gaming blogs turning this traffic into ad revenue.

One warning: the sales figures below are Sensor Tower estimates, reported by Reuters. Take-Two Interactive has not released official sales numbers yet.

GTA 6 Launch by the Numbers

  • Release date: Thursday, November 19, 2026, midnight local time.
  • Platforms: PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. No PC version announced.
  • GTA 6 review score: 99 on Metacritic (PS5, 150 critics, all positive). OpenCritic: 99, 99% recommend.
  • GTA 6 sales, day one: an estimated 24.58 million copies and about $2.36 billion (Sensor Tower).
  • GTA 6 price: $79.99 for the Standard Edition, $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition in the United States.
  • Streaming peak: 7,886,402 concurrent viewers across Twitch, YouTube, and Kick (Streams Charts, preliminary).
  • Internet record: DE-CIX data throughput hit 33.03 terabits per second on November 18, a new record.

GTA 6 Review Scores: 99 on Metacritic

The review embargo lifted at 11:00 UTC on November 18, 2026, the day before launch. Within twenty minutes, Metacritic showed 115 PS5 reviews at an average of 99 out of 100. By launch day, the count had grown to 150 critic reviews, and the score held at 99. Not one was mixed or negative, and the lowest score any critic gave the game was 90.

That 99 ties The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (1998) as the highest-rated game in Metacritic history. OpenCritic tells the same story: a Top Critic Average of 99 across 89 reviews, with 99 percent of critics recommending the game. Either way you measure it, this is the best-reviewed game of 2026, and one of the best-reviewed games ever.

The scores outlet by outlet

The perfect scores came from the biggest names in games journalism. IGN gave GTA 6 a 10 out of 10, with Luke Reilly calling it "the single most impressive video game I have ever played" after more than 120 hours of play. GameSpot also awarded 10 out of 10, praising Rockstar's dedication to small details. Game Informer gave it 10 out of 10 as well, and Forbes matched that with a 10.

The five-star reviews were just as emphatic. Eurogamer awarded five out of five, GamesRadar+ called it "the highest-fidelity sandbox in existence," and Video Games Chronicle gave five stars, calling it "a modern masterpiece" while noting minor performance issues in dense areas. The Guardian, Empire, and Giant Bomb each gave five out of five as well.

The scores that were not perfect were still very high. The Washington Post gave four stars out of five, the New York Times rated it 90 out of 100, and IGN Japan also gave it 90. Those are the lowest scores on record.

What did the critics praise? An open world with a density of detail nobody had built before, a story about Lucia and Jason that feels more human than the series has managed, driving that IGN called series-best, and lighting and traffic that make Leonida feel alive. The BBC reported that critics broadly agreed the game was an ambitious, groundbreaking achievement that justified the 13-year wait.

One number has not arrived yet: the Metacritic user score. User reviews opened at 12:00 Pacific Time on November 19. Player scores always land lower than critic scores for a game this big, because millions of people vote instead of 150 professionals. Check the user score separately. It is a different story, and your readers will search for both.

GTA 6 Sales: $2.36 Billion in a Single Day

Sensor Tower estimates GTA 6 sold 24.58 million copies from the start of pre-orders through its first day, generating about $2.36 billion for Take-Two Interactive. Reuters reported the figures on November 20. That is nearly three times the roughly $800 million that GTA 5 earned in its first 24 hours in September 2013, then the best first day in video game history.

GTA 5 sold 11.21 million copies on its first day and needed three days to pass $1 billion. GTA 6 sold about 2.2 times as many copies and made nearly three times the money, passing $1 billion on pre-orders alone.

PlayStation carried most of the sales: about 18.44 million copies on PS5, worth roughly $1.77 billion, against about 6.15 million copies worth $588 million on Xbox. That is roughly 75 percent PlayStation and 25 percent Xbox, though both splits are estimates, and Take-Two has not confirmed a platform breakdown.

How the pre-orders built to $2 billion

The day-one total was built through pre-orders, which opened in June 2026. Pre-orders passed $1 billion eight days before release, reached $1.54 billion by November 17, and stood at $1.84 billion by Thursday morning, already past the $1.8 billion worldwide box office gross of this year's film The Odyssey. Revenue rose by a further $520 million before the end of launch day.

Karl Kontus, Sensor Tower's senior vice president of video game insights, said GTA 6 "has captivated consumers, becoming 2026's best-selling game based on pre-orders alone." Take-Two said pre-orders were running far ahead of its initial expectations, and president Karl Slatoff called them "extraordinary." The publisher kept its fiscal 2027 net bookings forecast at $8 billion to $8.2 billion, unchanged.

The forecasts that came before launch now look cautious. Newzoo projected launch-week revenue of $3.3 billion to $5.2 billion, Piper Sandler forecast more than 45 million units at launch, and Niko Partners forecast worldwide sales above $3 billion this year. Bank of America had said the game needed at least $1.9 billion in its first three days to satisfy investors, and the estimated first-day total already clears that bar.

There is also a cost side to this story. Take-Two has spent an estimated $1 billion or more producing GTA 6, making it one of the most expensive entertainment projects ever made. Chief executive Strauss Zelnick has said Take-Two will share official sales data soon. When it does, treat the official number as the real record and the Sensor Tower estimate as the early signal.

How Much Does GTA 6 Cost? GTA 6 Price and Editions

The GTA 6 price is one of the most searched questions about the game. In the United States, the Standard Edition costs $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition costs $99.99, identical on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. That $79.99 standard price sits above the $69.99 AAA price point used for years, and below the rumored $100 base-game price.

Advertisement

There are only two editions, and the difference is a $20 upgrade. The Standard Edition at $79.99 is the complete game, including the Vintage Vice City Pack for purchases made before November 20, 2026. The Ultimate Edition at $99.99 adds the Ultimate Edition Upgrade: premium vehicles, weapons, apparel, and extra content tied to Jason and Lucia's story. Standard owners can buy the upgrade separately for $20, so the total is $99.99 either way.

Prices outside the United States follow the same two-tier structure: £69.99 and £89.99 in the United Kingdom, €79.99 and €99.99 in eurozone countries, CA$109.99 and CA$139.99 in Canada, AU$129.95 and AU$159.95 in Australia, and Rs 5,999 and Rs 7,499 in India. The prices match on PS5 and Xbox in almost every country.

Two buying details catch new players out. First, there is no disc: the physical edition is a boxed download code, and the Ultimate Edition is digital only. Second, the Rockstar Store sells a collector's box, The Goodtime State, Vice City Collection, for $399.99, which contains eleven objects and no game at all. For most people, the Standard Edition at $79.99 is the right buy, with the $20 upgrade later if they want the extras.

GTA 6 Broke Streaming Records Too

The sales records were only half the launch. The other half happened on Twitch, YouTube, and Kick, where GTA 6 drew the biggest livestreaming audience any game launch has ever seen. According to preliminary Streams Charts data, reported by Polygon, GTA 6 reached a combined peak of 7,886,402 concurrent viewers within about three hours of the game first becoming available, roughly 4.5 times the previous launch record.

The split across platforms shows how evenly the audience spread. Twitch peaked at about 3.29 million concurrent viewers, YouTube at about 3.19 million, and Kick at about 1.48 million. Twitch alone accounted for less than half of the combined total, because the launch rolled around the world region by region, and every region had its own streamers going live at midnight.

The Twitch number is a record in its own right. GTA 6's peak of 3.29 million makes it the most-watched gaming category in Twitch history, ahead of League of Legends at 3.11 million and Fortnite at 3.04 million. The previous record for a game launch belonged to Valorant's closed beta, which drew 1.74 million viewers in April 2020. GTA 6 nearly doubled it in its first hours. Twitch counted viewership up 111 percent week over week, with tens of millions of hours watched in the first couple of hours, the equivalent of more than 1,500 years of nonstop viewing.

Kick set its own record, with 1,128 channels streaming GTA 6 at once, beating Fortnite's mark of 887. The biggest streams came from creators on every continent: Indonesia's Windah Basudara led the early top ten, followed by IShowSpeed, Brazil's alanzoka, and Spain's Rubius. One streamer, HoneyMad, drew 642,000 viewers on her own.

The launch even broke the internet itself. DE-CIX said data throughput across its exchanges hit a new record of 33.03 terabits per second at 20:20 Central European Time on Wednesday, November 18, a jump of about 25 percent since the start of the year. Rockstar's official launch trailer, released on October 15, 2026, has more than 85 million views. Twitch chief executive Dan Clancy told Bloomberg Television on November 13 that he expects an even bigger moment next year, when multiplayer arrives. That is an expectation, not a confirmation, and Rockstar has not announced a multiplayer mode.

Inside Leonida: What Critics Loved About GTA 6 Gameplay

GTA 6 is set in Leonida, Rockstar's fictional version of Florida, with Vice City at its center. The two protagonists are Lucia and Jason, a pair whose story the critics describe as more human than anything the series has done before.

The praise falls into four buckets. The first is density: traffic that behaves like traffic, lighting that changes how a street feels, and side streets that reward players who wander. IGN called the driving series-best after more than 120 hours of play. GamesRadar+ called it the highest-fidelity sandbox in existence. The second bucket is the story: GameSpot praised leads who feel more human than past GTA heroes, and the Washington Post praised the attention to human behavior.

The third bucket is polish. Multiple reviews call the game "unusually polished" for its scale, and VGC's only real criticism was minor performance issues in dense areas. The game was delayed twice from late 2025, and the extra months show in the finished product. The fourth bucket is scale, and it is the hardest to write about honestly, because so much is still unverified. The GTA 6 map is the most searched gameplay question right now, and the honest answer is that Rockstar has not published a map size. What the reviews confirm is that Leonida is far larger than a single city: Vice City is the hub, but the state around it includes swamps, small towns, coastline, and open country. Do not invent a square-kilometer figure. A wrong number costs you the trust that brings readers back.

What Is Still Missing: PC, Online Mode, and the 2027 Question

The launch is incomplete in three ways, each one a content opportunity. First, there is no PC version. Rockstar's official platform list names only PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and no PC date exists. GTA 5 took about a year and a half to reach PC, and GTA 6 will likely follow a similar path, but that is a pattern, not a promise. The honest answer today is that there is no date.

Second, there is no online mode at launch. The game that came out on November 19 is a single-player experience, and GTA Online's successor has no launch date for GTA 6. Twitch's chief executive expects multiplayer in 2027, but Rockstar has not announced it. The multiplayer launch will be a second launch event with its own records and search wave. Blogs that are ready will earn twice from one game.

Third, there is no disc. The physical edition is a code in a box, which matters for collectors, for players with slow internet, and for resale. These are small details next to a $2.36 billion launch, but they are exactly the details readers search for after they buy the game.

What the GTA 6 Launch Means for Gaming Blogs

The GTA 6 launch is not just a story about Rockstar. It is the biggest traffic event the gaming web will see for years, and the blogs that handle it well will earn from it for months. Millions of people are searching for GTA 6 review scores, sales figures, prices, editions, maps, gameplay guides, and PC release news, all at the same time, and most of the pages answering those searches were written before launch, when the answers did not exist yet.

That gap is the opportunity. A post written after launch, with verified numbers and real answers, can outrank a pre-launch preview that guessed at the same questions. Nobody searches "when does GTA 6 come out" anymore. They search "what is the GTA 6 Metacritic score," "how much did GTA 6 make," "how much is GTA 6," and "is GTA 6 on PC." Each of those is a separate article, or a separate section of a long article like this one.

Updating is how news content wins. The Metacritic score was 99 from 115 reviews within twenty minutes of the embargo lifting, and 99 from 150 reviews by launch day. A blog that published a scores roundup on November 18 and updated it twice had three chances to rank for "GTA 6 review" searches. The sales story works the same way: Sensor Tower's $2.36 billion estimate is the headline today, but Take-Two's official figure will replace it, and the blog that updates first will win the second wave.

Traffic from the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia pays far more per thousand views than traffic from anywhere else. Three mistakes cost gaming blogs money on a launch like this. The first is publishing a thin post. A 300-word news rewrite cannot rank against a 3,000-word guide that answers the review, sales, price, and streaming questions in one place. The second is never updating. A GTA 6 post from launch week that still says "150 reviews" in February is a dead page. The third is chasing the spike and ignoring the tail. The launch spike is the biggest traffic a gaming blog will ever see, but the guide traffic that follows it is steadier, and the evergreen traffic around the PC release and online mode pays the bills next year.

What Happens Next: PC, Online Mode, and the Second Wave

The GTA 6 story is not over. The two biggest events after launch have no dates yet. The first is the PC release, which Rockstar has not announced. When it does, blogs with a PC page ready to update will rank first.

The second is online mode. GTA Online kept GTA 5 earning for more than a decade, and whatever Rockstar builds for GTA 6 will likely do the same. Twitch's chief executive expects multiplayer in 2027, but that is an expectation, not a confirmation from Rockstar. The online launch will turn a single-player story into a live service with weekly updates, events, and an economy that players need guides for.

Smaller waves are coming too: the first story DLC, the first major patch, the first holiday sale, and the first price drop will each produce their own search spikes. GTA 6 will be the most gifted game of the year, so "GTA 6 price" and "GTA 6 deals" searches will stay hot through December. Attention is finite, and the sites that follow it are the sites that grow.

GTA 6 is the biggest launch in entertainment history by almost any measure. A 99 on Metacritic from 150 critics, tying a record that stood since 1998. A streaming peak of nearly 7.9 million concurrent viewers, the largest a game launch has ever drawn. The numbers will move as Take-Two reports official figures and as the PC and online launches arrive.
