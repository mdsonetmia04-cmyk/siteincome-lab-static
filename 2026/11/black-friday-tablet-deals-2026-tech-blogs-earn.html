Black Friday Tablet Deals 2026: How Tech Blogs Turn the Year's Biggest Tablet Sale Into Ad Revenue

Black Friday 2026 falls on November 27, and the tablet deals are already live. Amazon kicked off its Black Friday Week on November 19, running through Cyber Monday on November 30, and Walmart put its Black Friday Deals online on November 23. The early prices are genuinely strong: the 11-inch iPad is selling for $399 against a $449 list price, the Samsung Galaxy Tab S11 is down to $699.99 from $999.99, and Amazon's brand-new Alexa Tablet 12 Pro has already been cut to $449.99 from $549.99. For shoppers, this is the cheapest week of the year to buy a tablet. For tech bloggers, it is one of the highest-traffic weeks of the year — and one of the best weeks to earn ad revenue from deal-hunting readers.

This guide does two things. First, it walks through the best Black Friday tablet deals of 2026 that are actually live right now, with the real prices and the retailers selling them. Second, it explains how tech blogs turn this deal traffic into ad revenue.

Why Tablets Are the Sleeper Hit of Black Friday 2026

Tablets rarely get the headlines that televisions and laptops get, but they sit at the perfect intersection of price and giftability: cheap enough to buy on impulse, expensive enough that a $100 or $300 saving feels real, and useful enough that almost everyone in a household wants one. A parent buys a kids tablet, a student buys an iPad for notes, a commuter buys a Galaxy Tab for movies, and a creative buys an iPad Pro for drawing.

Tablets are also a gift category, which means buyers are deciding now and comparing prices across several stores before they commit.

Apple iPad Deals: Small Discounts, Huge Demand

Apple never discounts deeply, and that is exactly why iPad deals get so much search traffic. Shoppers know the discount will be small, so they hunt harder for it. The best budget iPad deal of the season so far is the standard 11-inch iPad with the A16 chip, selling for $399 at Amazon against a $449 list price. That is only $50 off, but it matches the price seen during October Prime Day, and it is the cheapest way into a current-generation iPad. The 11-inch screen, the A16 chip, and the 12-megapixel cameras on the front and back make it a solid choice for video calls, streaming, and schoolwork.

The iPad Air is where the value sits this year. The 11-inch iPad Air with the M4 chip and 128GB of storage is selling for $649 at Amazon, down from $749. That is $100 off the newest Air, and the M4 chip makes it fast enough for photo editing, note-taking with the Apple Pencil, and serious multitasking. If you want the previous generation instead, the 11-inch iPad Air with the M2 chip is selling for $719 against an $899 list price — a $180 saving — and the newer M3 version is listed at $1,049 against $1,249, a $200 saving on a higher-storage configuration. Check the storage size on the listing before comparing prices, because iPad prices depend heavily on the configuration you pick.

The iPad mini is the compact pick. The iPad mini with the A17 Pro chip and 128GB of storage is selling for $499, down from $599, a $100 saving on the smallest iPad Apple makes. At 8.3 inches, it is the tablet for readers, commuters, and anyone who wants a device that fits in a jacket pocket. Apple's own event, November 27–30, offers a gift card of up to $300 with an eligible purchase instead of a price cut.

Samsung Galaxy Tab Deals: The Deepest Discounts of the Season

If Apple wins on demand, Samsung wins on discounts. The headline deal is the Galaxy Tab S11 with 128GB of storage, selling for $699.99 at Amazon against a $999.99 list price — a full $300 saving on Samsung's current flagship tablet. The 11-inch AMOLED display, the S Pen included in the box, and the microSD slot for extra storage make it the best Android tablet deal of the season for most people. It can reach up to 1,600 nits of brightness, so it stays readable outdoors, and the AMOLED screen makes movies look superb.

The Galaxy Tab S10 FE is the mid-range star. At $349.99 against a $549.99 list price, it is $200 off, and it gives buyers a large-screen Samsung tablet with S Pen support for roughly the price of a budget iPad. The larger FE+ model has also been reported at $200 off at Samsung's own store. Budget buyers should look at the Galaxy Tab A11+, selling for $199.99, down from $299.99 — a $100 saving that puts a capable Samsung tablet under $200 for the first time this season.

The biggest absolute saving in the whole tablet category belongs to the Galaxy Tab S10+ with 512GB of storage, listed at $799.97 against a $1,299.99 list price — a $500 cut, or about 38 percent off. That is a previous-generation flagship, which is why the discount is so deep, but the 12.4-inch display and 512GB of storage make it a serious machine for anyone who works on a tablet.

Amazon's New Alexa Tablets: The Fire Tablet Is Gone

The biggest tablet story of 2026 is not a price cut — it is a rebrand. Amazon announced its Alexa Tablet family on October 8 and released it in the United States on October 14. These tablets replace the Fire lineup entirely, and they run Android with the Google Play store, which means buyers are no longer limited to Amazon's own app store. That is a major change, and it is the first thing a tablet deal post should explain, because many shoppers still search for "Fire tablet deals" and have no idea the name has changed.

The flagship of the new family is the Alexa Tablet 12 Pro, and it is already on sale. Amazon has cut it to $449.99 from $549.99, a $100 saving on a tablet that only launched in October. It has a 12-inch 120Hz screen, 8GB of memory, and 128GB of storage, with an optional Nanomatte glass finish. Reviewers who have gone hands-on with it praise the screen and the battery life — one test found the tablet still had 43 percent battery left after 12 hours of use — and the Android operating system makes it far more versatile than the Fire tablets it replaces.

The standard Alexa Tablet 11 is the value pick. Amazon has cut it by up to $160 in early Black Friday listings, bringing the price below $220 in some configurations and below $250 in others. It has an 11-inch screen with a 2560 by 1600 resolution, 8GB of memory, and 64GB of storage, plus Google Play access. Parents should also know that the old Fire tablets have not vanished from the sale: the Fire HD 8 Kids tablet, for ages 3 to 7, is selling for $99.99 against a $179.99 list price — 44 percent off — with a kid-proof case and ad-free kids content included.

Lenovo, TCL, and the Rest: The Best Tablets Under $250

Below the big three brands, the 2026 Black Friday tablet sale is full of bargains. Lenovo is the standout. The Lenovo Yoga Tab Plus Gen 2 is selling for $599.99, down from $729.99, a $130 saving on a premium Android tablet. The Lenovo Legion Tab Gen 5, aimed at gamers, is selling for $699.99 against an $899.99 list price — $200 off a tablet built for mobile gaming. And at the budget end, the Lenovo Idea Tab with 128GB of storage is selling for $159.99, down from $199.99, which makes it one of the cheapest full-size tablets on sale this year. The Idea Tab Plus, also with 128GB, is listed at $229.99 against $294.99.

TCL is the dark horse of the season. The TCL NXTPAPER 11 Plus with 256GB of storage is selling for $227.98 against a $369.99 list price — a saving of just over $142 — and the larger TCL NXTPAPER 14 is selling for $329.99, down from $449.99. The NXTPAPER line is built around a paper-like display that is easier on the eyes for long reading sessions, which makes it a natural pick for students and heavy readers. The lesson for shoppers is simple: the best tablet deal is not always the biggest discount. A $40 saving on a $160 tablet matters more to a budget buyer than a $300 saving on a $1,000 tablet.

How to Tell a Real Tablet Deal From a Fake One

Not every Black Friday tablet price is a genuine bargain. Retailers can raise a starting price before the sale so the discount looks bigger, and deal roundups that simply repeat the retailer's numbers can mislead readers. The first check is the price history. The 11-inch iPad at $399 is a real deal because it matches the price seen during October Prime Day — it is a genuine low, not a number invented for November. The Galaxy Tab S11 at $699.99 is a real deal because the $300 saving is measured against a $999.99 list price that the tablet actually sold at.

Advertisement

The second check is the configuration. Tablet prices depend heavily on storage, connectivity, and accessories. The iPad Air M3 at $1,049 is a 1TB model with 5G cellular, not the base model — comparing it with the $649 M4 Air without noting the difference would mislead readers. The fourth check is the calendar. Prices change hourly during Black Friday week, so the best deal posts are updated every morning during sale week, with a visible "last updated" note. An updated post ranks better, earns more trust, and keeps readers coming back for the latest prices.

When to Buy: The Black Friday 2026 Sale Calendar

Timing is half the game in Black Friday tablet shopping. Amazon's Black Friday Week runs from November 19 through Cyber Monday on November 30, with new deals dropping daily and two drops a day — at midnight and 8 a.m. Pacific time — on Black Friday and Cyber Monday themselves. Most of the tablet prices in this guide come from Amazon, and the deepest cuts tend to land on those two drop days.

Walmart's Black Friday Deals run from November 23 through November 29. The sale went live online on November 23, with Walmart+ members getting early access to the hottest deals. In stores, the sale starts on November 27 at 6 a.m. local time, and Walmart's Cyber Monday deals run from November 30 through December 2. Target's Black Friday sale runs from November 22 through November 29, in stores, online, and in the app, with new weekend deals dropping from November 26 through November 29. Target stores open at 6 a.m. local time on Black Friday, and the first 100 eligible customers at every store that day receive a Target gift card worth $25, $50, or $100, with one customer at each store receiving a $1,000 gift card.

Best Buy's Black Friday Sale runs from November 19 through November 28, with stores open from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. on Black Friday itself, and its Cyber Sunday and Monday deals run from November 29 through November 30. All of the big retailers — Walmart, Target, Costco, Lowe's, Kohl's, Home Depot, and Best Buy — have confirmed they will be closed on Thanksgiving, November 26, 2026, so the online sales are the only game in town that day.

How Tech Blogs Turn Tablet Deal Traffic Into Ad Revenue

So how does a tablet deal post actually make money? The answer is display advertising. A reader searches for a tablet deal, lands on your post, and sees ads as they scroll. Every ad impression earns a small amount of money, and every click earns more. During Black Friday week, two things happen at once: traffic spikes because millions of people are searching for deals, and ad rates climb because advertisers are bidding harder to reach shoppers who are ready to buy. More readers times higher rates equals the best earning week of the year for many tech blogs.

Advertisement

The formats matter. A popunder ad opens behind the browser window and earns from the impression when the reader eventually sees it. Banner ads — the familiar 728 by 90 leaderboard at the top of the page and the 300 by 250 rectangle inside the article — earn from every view. Native banners blend into the page and tend to get clicked more often. The blogs that earn the most from Black Friday do not rely on one format; they run several, placed where readers actually look: above the fold, at the top of the article, in the middle of the deal list, and below the article.

But formats are only half the story. The other half is traffic quality. A tablet deal post attracts readers with purchase intent, and advertisers pay more to reach them than they pay to reach casual browsers. That is why a deal post with 1,000 readers can earn more than a general tech post with 5,000 readers. Finally, there is the referral angle. Ad networks like Adsterra run referral programs that pay publishers a share of the earnings of every publisher they refer, for life. A post that explains how to monetize a website and includes a referral link can keep earning long after Black Friday ends.

The Blogger Playbook: How to Write a Tablet Deal Post That Ranks

Writing a deal post that actually ranks on Google follows a repeatable playbook. The first rule is to publish early. The tablet deal posts that rank best on Black Friday are usually the ones published days before the sale starts, because Google needs time to find, crawl, and trust a new page. A post published on November 23 has four days to climb the rankings before the Black Friday search spike on November 27. A post published on November 27 itself is already late.

The second rule is to lead with the price. Readers searching for tablet deals want numbers, not introductions. The best deal posts put the price in the headline or in the first paragraph, and they put a comparison table near the top of the page so a reader can see every deal in ten seconds. The third rule is to be specific. Name the exact model, the exact configuration, the exact price, the exact saving, and the exact retailer. "iPad on sale" tells the reader nothing. "11-inch iPad (A16) for $399 at Amazon, $50 off the $449 list price" tells the reader everything.

The fifth rule is to update daily. A deal post is a living document during Black Friday week. Prices change, tablets sell out, new deals appear, and a post that still shows yesterday's prices is a post that misleads its readers. Update the prices every morning and note the date of the update. Link to your other Black Friday posts — your Black Friday laptop deals guide, your Black Friday smartphone deals guide, and your Black Friday Apple deals guide — and each of those posts should link back. Internal links are the cheapest traffic a blog can buy, because they cost nothing and they keep readers inside your site.

Mistakes That Cost Bloggers Money Every Black Friday

The first mistake is publishing stale prices. Nothing destroys a deal blog's credibility faster than a reader clicking through to Amazon and finding a different price from the one your post promised. Check every price on the morning you publish, and check again the next morning. If a deal has expired, remove it or mark it clearly as expired.

The third mistake is ignoring the long tail. Everyone writes about the iPad and the Galaxy Tab S11. Far fewer bloggers write about the TCL NXTPAPER, the Lenovo Idea Tab, or the reMarkable Paper Pro — and the readers searching for those tablets have almost no competition for their attention. A paragraph on a niche tablet can outrank a whole post on a popular one. The blogs that earn the most from deal season are the ones that publish early, update daily, and promote their posts on social media, in newsletters, and in every related article on their site.

The Bottom Line

Black Friday 2026 is the best week of the year to buy a tablet, and the prices prove it. The 11-inch iPad at $399 is the cheapest way into Apple's world, the Galaxy Tab S11 at $699.99 is the deepest flagship discount of the season, and Amazon's new Alexa Tablet 12 Pro at $449.99 is the most interesting new tablet on sale. The smart move is to decide your budget first, pick the tablet that fits it, and buy before the model you want sells out — because the best deals of Black Friday week rarely survive until Cyber Monday. The sale ends on November 30. The audience you earn this week does not have to.
