Costco Black Friday Deals 2026: 98-Inch TCL TV for $999.97, MacBook Air M4 at $749.99 and Dyson Airwrap $479.99 — How Tech Blogs Turn the Warehouse Giant's Biggest Sale Into Ad Revenue

Black Friday 2026 is officially here, and while Amazon, Walmart and Target have spent the whole month shouting about doorbusters, Costco has been quietly running the most disciplined sale in retail. No flash-sale countdown timers. No website crashing at midnight. Just a warehouse full of 98-inch TVs for $999.97, a MacBook Air with Apple's M4 chip for $749.99, and a Dyson Airwrap for $479.99 — all behind a $65 membership wall that keeps the crowds smaller and the deals real. If you have ever wondered whether the warehouse club is worth it for one weekend a year, this is the weekend that answers the question.

Costco's Black Friday weekend runs from Friday, November 27 through Monday, November 30, 2026, inside a wider Holiday Savings event that started October 30 and runs through November 30. The Holiday Savings book lists roughly 277 deals across the season, with a concentrated Black Friday weekend slate of about 46 deals — 22 available both in warehouses and online, 19 online-only, and 5 warehouse-only. Warehouses were closed on Thanksgiving, November 26, and they open on Black Friday at 9 a.m. for Executive members and 10 a.m. for Gold Star and Business members, closing at 8:30 p.m., though hours vary by warehouse. This guide covers the verified prices, the membership math, and the shopping strategy that gets you the big-ticket items before they sell out — plus what this sale teaches anyone trying to make money from a blog.

Why Costco's Black Friday Works Differently From Every Other Store

Every other retailer treats Black Friday as a single chaotic day. Costco treats it as the final wave of a month-long event, and that difference matters more than most shoppers realize. The Holiday Savings event runs in waves: Wave 2, the biggest batch of electronics, ran November 16–30 and covered earbuds, speakers, soundbars, tablets, smartwatches, security cameras, gaming laptops and monitors, plus another Apple Savings Event. Thanksgiving Day brought an online-only list while the warehouses were closed. The Black Friday weekend wave runs November 27–30 in warehouses and online, and Cyber Monday, November 30, is online only. Most prices run through November 30, some appliance prices through December 2, and valid dates vary by item — offers last while supplies last.

That structure is the first thing to understand. Costco does not discount everything a little; it discounts a small number of high-ticket, brand-name items a lot. Televisions, laptops, vacuums and appliances get the meaningful price drops. Everyday staples barely move. The warehouse model makes that possible: Costco carries a narrow selection, buys in enormous volume, and passes the savings to members instead of spending money on advertising. It is also why the deals feel different from Amazon's. Amazon lists millions of deals and expects you to hunt. Costco lists 46 Black Friday weekend deals and expects you to decide fast, because the warehouse only stocks what it stocks, and when the pallet is empty, the deal is over.

The second difference is the membership gate. You need a Costco membership to buy in a warehouse, and online, nonmembers can buy some items but typically pay about 5% more. A Gold Star membership costs $65 a year, and the Executive membership is $130 a year with a 2% annual reward on eligible purchases, capped at $1,250 a year. The breakeven point on Executive is about $3,250 of annual spending — spend more than that and the upgrade pays for itself; spend less and Gold Star is the right call. Executive members also get in an hour earlier on Black Friday, which is a genuine advantage when the warehouse opens and the TV pallets are full. That one hour is often the difference between a 98-inch TV and a story about the 98-inch TV you almost bought.

The third difference is the warranty. Costco pairs its TV prices with free two-year Allstate and Concierge warranty coverage and a 90-day return window on electronics, where most retailers give you 30 days and try to sell you an extended warranty at the register. The LG 65-inch C5 OLED, for example, comes bundled with an Allstate three-year protection plan, giving five years of total coverage. When you are spending $1,399.99 on a television, that bundled protection is worth real money, and it is one of the reasons Costco's TV prices are often the lowest effective price in the country even when another store matches the sticker price.

The TV Deals: A 98-Inch Screen for Under $1,000 Leads the Sale

Televisions are the heart of Costco's Black Friday, and this year's list is the strongest the warehouse has run. The headline is the TCL 98-inch Q6-Series 4K QLED Smart TV at $999.97, valid through December 1. A 98-inch screen for under a thousand dollars would have been unthinkable five years ago, and it is the single most shareable deal in the entire sale — the kind of price that stops a scroll, starts a group chat, and sells out a pallet before noon. If you want one, be at the warehouse when the doors open, because Costco does not restock a Black Friday pallet once it is gone.

The OLED wall is where the serious home-theater buyers should look. The Samsung 77-inch S90F Series OLED 4K TV is $1,498 with a five-year warranty included, and the Samsung 65-inch OLED TV is $1,399. The LG 65-inch C5 Series OLED, the newest 2025 model, is $1,399.99 and includes the Allstate three-year protection plan bundle for five years of total coverage. The Sony 65-inch Bravia 8 OLED is $1,499. The LG C5 at $1,399.99 with five years of coverage is the best value of the four for most buyers, because the bundled warranty removes the one cost that usually follows an OLED purchase.

The mid-range is just as strong. The TCL 65-inch QM6K Pro QD-Mini LED TV is $499.99 for November 27–30, and the Hisense 100-inch QD7 Hi-QLED TV is $999.99 in the same window — a hundred-inch screen for the price of a good 65-inch OLED. The Sony BRAVIA 6 4K OLED TV is $999.99 for the 55-inch and $1,199.99 for the 65-inch online, valid through November 30. The TCL Q77L 4K QLED TV runs $259.99 to $379.99 depending on size for November 27–30. And the budget picks are almost absurd: a Samsung 65-inch Crystal UHD 4K Smart TV for $300, and the Samsung 65-inch U8000H Crystal UHD 4K TV for $299.99. The Hisense 65-inch U65 Pro Mini-LED 4K TV with a five-year coverage bundle is $529.99, down from $600.

How do these prices compare with the rest of the market? Our Black Friday TV deals guide tracked the Samsung S90H OLED at $1,397.99 and the LG C6 at $1,349.99 at other retailers, so Costco's $1,498 for the 77-inch S90F is the bigger-screen play at a comparable per-inch price, while its $300 Samsung 65-inch Crystal UHD undercuts almost every competing 65-inch deal this year. The pattern is consistent: Costco wins on the very large screens and the very cheap screens, and it wins on warranty everywhere else. Before you buy, measure your wall and your car.

Laptops and Apple Deals: MacBook Air M4 for $749.99

Costco's laptop deals are quieter than its TV deals, but the MacBook Air 13-inch with Apple's M4 chip at $749.99 is the standout. An early Holiday Savings window listed the MacBook Air 13-inch at $1,099.99, so the Black Friday weekend price represents a sharp drop from the warehouse's own earlier listing. Costco is also running an Apple Savings Event for November 27–30 with a limit of two per member, though it had not published discount amounts for every item at the time of writing, so check the warehouse listing before you go.

The Windows side is built for value. The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 3X laptop is $499 for the Black Friday window, and the Lenovo IdeaPad 15.6-inch touchscreen laptop is $149.99, valid through November 27 — the lowest price seen for that machine, and a genuine gift-tier laptop for a student or a parent who just needs a working computer. The Lenovo gaming laptop with an Nvidia GeForce RTX 5060 is $1,199, which is a real entry point into PC gaming at a price that would have bought a mid-range card alone two years ago. The HP OmniBook 5 16-inch laptop is $500 off for November 27–30, the Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5i with 16GB of memory and a 1TB drive is $300 off, the HP OmniBook X 14-inch is $400 off with a limit of two, and the HP OmniBook 3 17.3-inch is $350 off, all valid through November 30. Costco does not show a regular price on many of these listings, so the dollar-off figures are the honest way to judge them — and $500 off a 16-inch HP is a serious discount by any measure.

The smart play with Costco laptops is the return policy. Electronics carry a 90-day return window, which is three times the industry standard, and that matters when you are buying a laptop as a gift. If the recipient already has one, or wants a different size, you have until late February to bring it back. No other major retailer gives you that much room, and it is one of the quiet reasons Costco's laptop prices are worth taking seriously even when another store lists the same model a few dollars cheaper.

Dyson Deals: The Airwrap Leads a Deep Bench

If there is one brand Costco discounts harder than any other on Black Friday, it is Dyson. The Dyson Airwrap Multistyler Complete Long is $479.99, valid through December 1, and the Dyson Airwrap i.d. with Chitosan post-style serum is $499.99, down from $649.99 — a $150 saving on the hair tool that tops nearly every holiday gift guide. The Dyson Supersonic hair dryer is $359.99 with its V9 motor, attachments and stand, valid through December 1, with some bundles including extra attachments. For context, our Target Black Friday guide found the Dyson V9 at $269.99, so Target wins on that one model — but Costco's Airwrap prices are the ones to beat, and the warehouse stocks the Long barrel version that many retailers do not carry.

The vacuum lineup is just as deep. The Dyson Gen5detect Extra cordless stick vacuum is $649.99 after a $240 discount, valid through November 30. The Dyson V15 Detect Total Clean Extra is $699.99, with a 60-minute runtime and the laser fluffy head that reveals microscopic dust. The Dyson V9 Submarine Extra wet and dry cordless vacuum is $329.99 with seven-in-one functions, valid through November 27. The Dyson Ball Animal Optic upright vacuum is $299.99 after a $100 discount, valid through November 30. If you are buying a Dyson as a gift, the Airwrap i.d. at $499.99 is the safest pick — it is the product people ask for by name, and the $150 saving is easy to verify against the regular price.

Home Security, Smart Home and Outdoor Gear

Costco's home-security deals are the quiet value of the sale, because they bundle hardware that usually costs far more when bought piece by piece. The SimpliSafe 10-piece home security system with an Outdoor Camera Series 2 is $99.99, a full $100 off. The Blink Whole Home 2K+ security camera bundle is $99.99 as well. Two complete home-security setups for a hundred dollars each is the kind of deal that makes a renter or a first-time homeowner stop scrolling. The NEX Playground game console is $289.99 after a $60 discount, and the Jackery 600 v2 solar generator is $100 off — a practical pick for anyone who has ever lost power in a winter storm and wished they had a backup. The GoPro HERO13 Black action camera bundle is $339.99, a solid price for a camera that usually sells at full price through the holidays.

These are the deals that reward the shopper who walks past the TV wall and looks at the middle aisles. If home security is on your list, buy it on Friday morning rather than Sunday afternoon, because the Black Friday weekend list only has 46 deals and the security bundles are two of the cheapest tickets in the whole sale.

Kitchen and Appliances: The Big-Ticket Savings

The kitchen deals start with a classic. The KitchenAid Artisan Plus 5-quart stand mixer with a pasta attachment is $459.99, down from $599.99, and the Cuisinart 13-cup food processor is $149.99 with its blades and attachments included, valid through November 27. The KitchenAid is the mixer that appears in every holiday baking list, and a $140 saving on a machine that lasts decades is the definition of a good Black Friday buy.

Appliances are where the dollar savings get largest. Samsung Bespoke four-door refrigerators start at $1,999 with up to $1,100 off, and the Whirlpool 24-inch dishwasher is $499.99, a $500 saving, on the Cyber Monday online-only list. Some appliance prices run through December 2, past the end of the Black Friday weekend, so if you are remodeling a kitchen, the warehouse gives you a few extra days that other retailers do not.

The Membership Math: Is $65 Worth It for One Weekend?

This is the question every nonmember asks, and the honest answer is that it depends on what you buy. A Gold Star membership costs $65 a year, and the Executive membership is $130 a year with a 2% annual reward capped at $1,250. The breakeven on Executive is roughly $3,250 of annual spending. But for a single Black Friday weekend, the math is simpler: buy the 98-inch TCL at $999.97 and you have saved several hundred dollars against the same screen elsewhere; buy the MacBook Air M4 at $749.99 and the saving covers the membership several times over; buy the Airwrap i.d. at $499.99 and the $150 saving alone pays for two years of Gold Star. The membership pays for itself the moment you buy one big-ticket item at a real discount, and it keeps paying every time you shop for the rest of the year.

There is also the nonmember route. Online, nonmembers can buy some items but typically pay about 5% more, which on a $1,498 television is roughly $75 — more than the cost of a Gold Star membership. Joining is the cheaper option. And if you already have a membership, the Executive upgrade is worth considering before Friday: the 2% reward applies to your Black Friday purchases, and the 9 a.m. early entry is worth real money when the TV pallets are full and the regular members are still outside. One more point in Costco's favor: the warehouse allows price-adjustment requests for costco.com orders within the promotion date, so if a price drops further during the sale window, you can ask for the difference back instead of returning the item and buying it again.

How to Shop Costco Black Friday Like a Pro

First, check the list before you go. Costco's Holiday Savings page lists the items, prices and valid dates by window, and the Black Friday weekend deals are split between warehouse, online and both. Nineteen of the 46 weekend deals are online-only, so if you only walk the warehouse, you will miss nearly half the sale. Second, go early on Friday. Executive members enter at 9 a.m. and everyone else at 10 a.m., and the big TVs and the Airwrap bundles move first. Third, buy the big items in the warehouse and the small ones online. A 98-inch TV is a warehouse purchase — you can see the box, confirm the model, and load it yourself. A security camera bundle or a food processor is an online purchase, and it will arrive while you are still deciding whether you need it.

Fourth, use the return policy as your safety net. Electronics get 90 days, and the warehouse's satisfaction guarantee covers most other items for far longer than any competitor. That means you can buy the gift now and decide later, which is the entire point of shopping early in the holiday season.

What This Sale Teaches Bloggers About Making Money

Costco's Black Friday is a masterclass in the exact thing this blog exists to teach: how attention turns into revenue. The warehouse does not win by having the most deals. It wins by having a small number of deals that are so good they get shared — a 98-inch TV for $999.97, a MacBook Air for $749.99, an Airwrap for $479.99. Every one of those prices is a headline, a social post, a group-chat message. That is the same mechanic that makes a blog post earn money. A post nobody clicks earns nothing, no matter how good it is. A post built around one verifiable, shareable number — a price, a saving, a comparison — gets clicked, and clicks are what ad networks pay for.

The second lesson is trust. Costco can charge $65 just to walk in the door because members trust that the prices inside are real. Bloggers earn the same kind of trust by verifying every price they publish and dropping the claims they cannot verify, and the traffic follows the trust. The third lesson is the membership model itself: Costco's best customers pay for access, and the best blogs build an audience that comes back without being asked. The sale ends on November 30.

Costco's Black Friday 2026 is the most disciplined sale of the season: 46 weekend deals, led by a 98-inch TCL TV for $999.97, a MacBook Air M4 for $749.99, and a Dyson Airwrap for $479.99, all running November 27–30 with a 90-day electronics return window and bundled warranties that other retailers charge extra for. The warehouse rewards the shopper who plans — and this year, the plan is worth more than ever.
